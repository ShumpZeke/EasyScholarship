# Treasure Chest Visual Style Guide

## Palette
- obsidian black
- graphite gray
- aged dark wood
- brushed gold
- warm amber
- ruby red
- emerald green
- sapphire blue
- pearl white
- smoky gray

## Materials
- aged wood: rough, dark, realistic grain
- metal bands: brushed gold, worn edges
- coins: high metalness, varied roughness
- gems: translucent colored material with sparkle
- cards: glassy translucent scholarship documents
- fog: warm near chest, cool in landscape

## UI
- dark glass panels
- gold borders
- cream text
- clean modern typography
- no cartoon pirate theme
