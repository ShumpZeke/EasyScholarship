# Animation Plan

## Scroll camera beats
1. Establish landscape and chest
2. Push toward chest
3. Orbit slightly to show chest materials
4. Zoom to latch area
5. Lid opens
6. Camera enters warm gold glow
7. Treasure and scholarship cards reveal

## Micro animations
- chest idle sway
- latch glow pulse
- lid pre-open vibration
- particles increase near open moment
- gems sparkle
- cards float upward
- coins shimmer subtly

## Route animations
- home to login: camera/overlay moves to side panel
- login to signup: form panel slides/rotates softly
- auth to home: return to chest scene
- signup to dashboard: glow transition into dashboard
