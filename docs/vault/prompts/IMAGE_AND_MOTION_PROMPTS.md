## 1) First frame image prompt

A centered black-and-gold futuristic scholarship vault sits on a circular pedestal in a smooth gray landscape with soft rolling hills, subtle fog, a pale misty sky, and a lot of clean negative space. The vault is closed, symmetrical, and perfectly centered. Matte black panels, thin gold trim, a circular locking detail in the center, calm luxury mood, simple environment, realistic materials, soft shadows, clean composition, no text, no logos, no people, wide 16:9 composition.

## 2) Last frame image prompt
A minimal cinematic 3D scene inspired by a premium landscape website hero. A black and gold futuristic vault is open in the center of the frame, perfectly centered and symmetrically composed. The camera is zoomed in closer so the vault fills much more of the image. Inside the vault, a warm golden glow shines outward with soft fog and mist. A few floating translucent scholarship cards hover inside the vault, glowing softly. The environment is very simple and clean, with smooth gray terrain, distant soft hills, a pale misty sky, and lots of empty negative space. The overall style is minimal, premium, calm, and polished. Realistic materials, subtle lighting, soft shadows, clean composition, no clutter, no people, no text, no logos, no extra objects, high-end 3D render, centered object, simple background, wide 16:9 composition.

## 3) Video prompt
Cinematic 8-second 3D animation of a black-and-gold scholarship vault in the center of a minimal gray landscape. Wide composition. The vault begins closed on a circular pedestal in a calm misty terrain with soft hills and a pale hazy sky. The camera slowly pushes forward like a premium website scroll interaction. Gold seams and the central lock begin to glow, subtle circular lock motion appears, then the vault doors slowly open. Warm muted gold light and soft fog spill outward. A few translucent scholarship cards float inside the open vault. End on a zoomed-in open-vault frame. Minimal, premium, centered, clean, quiet luxury, realistic materials, no text, no logos, no people.
